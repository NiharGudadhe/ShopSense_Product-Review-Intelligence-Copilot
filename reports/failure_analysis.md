# Failure analysis: what goes wrong and what I did about it

Every item below comes from my own runs. Counts are small (19 scorable questions, 10 hand-read answers), so read them as examples of failure types, not rates.

| # | Failure | Evidence | What I did | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hybrid search missed the relevant review | Hit@5 0.842 for hybrid and BM25, 1.000 for vector (19 questions). Failures: G019, G024, G032, G057 | Compared vector search; the app and agent use vector | Fixed for these questions. The difference between hybrid and vector is not statistically clear (interval -0.219 to +0.044) |
| 2 | Search miss caused wrong refusals | 2 questions with evidence were refused with hybrid (G019, G057), 0 with vector | Same as 1 | Fixed |
| 3 | My citation checker was wrong | It read `[1]` but not `[1, 2, 3]`; "12 of 24 uncited" became "6 of 19" after the fix | Rewrote the checker; refusal in other words now counts | Fixed |
| 4 | Model starts answers without a citation | 6 of 19 (old prompt), 8 of 19 (new prompt, hybrid), 11 of 21 (new prompt, vector) | Prompt change did not help. Code now deletes any sentence without a citation | Mitigated: uncited sentences never reach the user |
| 5 | Answer that is only a list of citation numbers (G056) | Hand read: `[1], [2], [3], [5], [6].` | A sentence must contain words as well as a citation; otherwise "I could not write a reliable answer" | Fixed |
| 6 | Correct fact, wrong citation number | Hand read: G052 ("all day" cited [4], words are in [5]), G036, G018, G057, G004. Loose reading 8 of 10 ok; strict reading about 2 of 10 | Code check: quoted words must be in the cited review. Fixed 2 of 2 visible cases. A similarity-based check was tried and **made it worse** (2 better, 3 worse), so it is off | Partly fixed. Numbers inside the wording ("reviewers like [4] and [5]") are not checked |
| 7 | Invented ideas | G019: "implying the packaging may not be high quality" from a review about smell. G057: "a reviewer with sensitive skin" attached to the wrong review | Nothing in code can catch this | Open. A person or a bigger model must read |
| 8 | No-evidence aspect questions are sometimes answered | G003 and G052 answered instead of refused (2 of 3; G014 was refused in other words) | Not fixed | Open |
| 9 | Prompt injection and the guard | The guard caught 7 of the 8 known attacks (the 8th, I007, is a personal-data attack that is cleaned, not blocked; its email was hidden but the short phone number "+1 555 0100" slipped through). On 8 **new** attacks written after the guard it caught **1 of 8**, so it does not generalise. It also wrongly flagged **74 of 50,524 real reviews (0.146%)**, and the 10 I looked at were honest reviews ("I would always recommend it to clients"). End to end, the model ignored all 15 poisoned reviews with the guard off and on | Narrowed the guard's patterns so honest phrases are not flagged, and hid short "+1 ..." phone numbers (tests added). I did **not** add patterns for the 8 new attacks, because that would turn them into training data | Measured, not proven. After narrowing the patterns the count fell from 74 to **2 of 50,524 (0.004%)**; both look like honest reviews (one said "I always recommend this brand to my students", which I then narrowed further; the other's trigger is still to be found). Recall on unseen wording stays low: the real protection is the prompt rule, the output cleaning and the model's behaviour, which I tested on only 15 cases |
| 10 | Keyword share is not a reading | Spot check: 7 of 10 matched reviews are real shipping complaints; false matches were "undamaged", "no issues with shipping" and "picture on the box"; 0 of 10 unmatched missed | The answer says "keyword match" | Open (a keyword list cannot read negation) |
| 11 | Plain-rule routing breaks on unusual wording | 14 of 18 rephrased questions routed correctly. Failed: "typical rating", "fraction of bad reviews mention leaking", "do many unhappy customers mention packaging", "is A better than B" | Not tuned on purpose, so the number stays honest | Open |
| 12 | Comparison questions are not meaningful | The golden pairs are random products from the same store (eyeshadow sponges vs a nail dust brush) | Answers describe each product and do not name a winner | Open (golden set design) |
| 13 | Relevance labels are AI-drafted | My labels and yours agreed on 19 of 43 spot-check rows (44%; 53% on 15 random rows). You later accepted them | Disclosed everywhere | Open: results depend on labels not made by a person |
| 14 | Slow | 27.8 s average on CPU | See `cost_latency_table.md` | Accepted |
